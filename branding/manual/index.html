01The logo and its parts

The AT-SUIT logo uses the same build as every AT brand: an orange gear shaped like a C, the letters AT inside it, and the product name, SUIT, to the right. Every part has a thick black outline.

Primary logo (wordmark): gear + AT + SUIT
Icon: gear + AT
Stacked: icon above AT SUIT

How the mark is built

1. Black outline
2. White band
3. Orange gear
4. AT letters
  • The gear is a ring with 8 round notches, one every 45°. The right side is cut open by two straight edges, which turns the gear into a C.
  • The layers, from outside in: a thick black outline, a thin white band, then the orange body.
  • AT is set in bold rounded-square capitals, white with a black outline. It sits inside the ring, and the T reaches towards the opening.
  • SUIT uses the same letters. It is centred on the gear and its cap height is about one third of the gear's height, the same as AT GROUP and AT EVENTS.
  • Lettering is Saira ExtraBold, converted to outlines. The logo files need no font. Don't retype the logo.

02The AT family

Every AT company uses the same gear and letters. Only the colour of the gear changes. AT-SUIT is orange, a colour no other member uses, so it is easy to tell apart when it appears next to its siblings.

Where AT-SUIT sits

AT-SUIT is a product brand. It is the software that AT GROUP and AT EVENTS crews use to run rooms at events. It is not a separate company. On materials that show several brands, put AT GROUP first, at the top or on the left, and give the other brands equal size below it or to the right.

When AT-SUIT appears next to AT EVENTS (for example, on a crew screen), keep both logos the same height and leave at least two A-heights between them.

Rules that apply to every member

  • The gear geometry, letter style and black outline never change between brands.
  • Each brand keeps its own colour. Never put another member's colour on the AT-SUIT gear.
  • The family uses the same plate rule on photos and busy backgrounds: put the logo on a dark navy plate.

04Clear space and minimum sizes

The unit of measure is x, the height of the "A" in the logo. Keep at least 1x of empty space on every side of the logo. Text, edges, photos and other logos all stay outside this space.

Orange dashed line = the minimum clear area. The shaded squares are 1x.

x = the cap height of the A. For the icon on its own, use the A in the icon. In the wordmark, x is about one third of the gear's height.

05Approved versions

Use the full-colour logo wherever you can. Because of its black outline, the same file works on both light and dark backgrounds. Use the one-colour versions only when colour is not possible: engraving, stamps, single-colour vinyl, fax or watermarks.

One-colour icon and stacked versions

In the one-colour versions, the gear and the letters are each one solid shape, separated by a small gap. These versions come in pure black or pure white only. Don't make them in orange or any other colour.

06Backgrounds and placement

Navy is the logo's home. On plain dark or plain light backgrounds, place the full-colour logo directly. On photos, video, patterns or anything busy, put the logo on a navy plate (#0B1020, at least 90% opaque) with 1x of padding around it.

Do · navy
Do · App Dark
Do · white / light grey
Do · busy background with navy plate

Placement

  • Web and app headers: put the icon or wordmark at the top left, vertically centred in the bar. The usual height is 28–40 px.
  • Room screens and overlays: place the icon in a corner, no taller than 6% of the screen height. It must never cover the timer, captions or presenter.
  • Documents: place the wordmark at the top left of the first page, 40–60 mm wide.
  • Banners and social images: centre the logo on navy, with the orange glow coming from one corner.

Mid-tones and orange

Mid-grey backgrounds are fine, because the black outline separates the logo from them. Never place the logo on an orange or warm-orange background, because the gear disappears into it. If the background has to be orange, use the white one-colour logo.

07App icon

The app icon is the logo's icon version with no extra plate, so it shows on whatever background the system uses. Two sources cover every size: the full icon for 48 px and above, and the simplified small icon for 16–40 px.

icon-512 / icon.ico (48–256)
apple-touch-icon (navy plate)
tray-16, tray-32 (simplified)
AT-SUIT · Control

AT-SUIT
Desktop shortcut (icon.ico)

Rules

  • Use the files as they are. Don't add rounded squares, shadows or gloss. On iOS, the apple-touch-icon already has a navy plate, because iOS fills transparent areas with black.
  • On Windows, icon.ico contains 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px. The 16, 24 and 32 px sizes use the simplified small icon.
  • For the PWA / web manifest, use icon-192.png and icon-512.png.
  • On Linux, use icon-512.png or icon-1024.png for the system tray and the .desktop file.
  • Never put a word next to the app icon inside the icon file. The OS shows the app name.
Web: <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">, with /favicon.ico as a fallback and <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">.

08Do's and don'ts

The logo only works if every copy is the same. Always use the supplied files. Never redraw or edit the logo.

Don't recolour the gear in another family colour (or any other colour)
Don't stretch, squash or skew the logo
Don't drop the black outline
Don't remove the white band (except in the small app icon)
Don't place on busy backgrounds without the navy plate
AT Suit
Don't retype the letters in another font
Don't colour the letters; they are always white with a black outline
Don't rotate it or add shadows, glows or bevels
Do use the supplied SVG and PNG files, keep 1x of clear space, use navy as the home background, use the small icon below 48 px, and use the white one-colour version on orange or photos where a plate is impossible.
Don't rearrange the parts (for example, SUIT above the gear), change the spacing, add a tagline inside the clear space, outline it a second time, or use the old neon "AT Live Caption" style logo for AT-SUIT.
