AT-SUIT: tech quick start

Your room's timer, cues, chat, help and captions in one workspace.

1Start your day

  • Open AT-SUIT Node. It starts with Windows.
  • Type your name and pick your room.
  • Choose Main PC for the laptop on the projector, or Backup PC for the one beside you.
  • No password: the laptop is already enrolled.

2Main or Backup

  • Main PC never shows a pop-up or a notification.
  • Backup PC shows chat, urgent messages and help calls bottom-right, over full-screen slides. They never make a sound and never take the keyboard.
  • Change it any time in This laptop.

3Run the timer

  • GO ▶ loads the next cue and starts it.
  • Start / pause, Stop, ◀ Prev, Next ▶.
  • Quick timers: tap a number of minutes. 🕒 Clock shows the time of day on stage.
  • Quick messages show on the stage screens; tick Blink to make them flash.

4The cue list

  • Add a cue for each session, with its length.
  • Put them in order with ↑ ↓.
  • Each cue says what happens at zero: keep going into overtime, stop, load the next or play the next.

5Chat and help

  • Chat: talk to your room, the whole crew or one person.
  • Something wrong? Press Help, pick Audio, Video, Presenter… say what's wrong and Send. The crew sees it at once.
  • When someone answers you'll see On my way; it closes when it's Resolved.
  • Handover notes: leave anything the next tech should know. Read what the last shift left; a note stays until someone presses Done.

6Captions

  • Press Send my mic to caption your room from this laptop's audio input.
  • Check the words appear, then leave it running.
  • Open captions shows what the audience sees.

7Arrange your screen

  • Every part is a window. Drag a window by its title onto the guides to dock it beside another or add it as a tab; let go anywhere else and it floats.
  • – minimise, □ maximise (or double-click the title), ✕ close. The taskbar at the bottom brings any window back.
  • ⧉ pops a window out onto a second monitor. Close it to put it back.
  • Layouts on the taskbar saves an arrangement you like; Reset to the starting layout puts every window back.

!If something goes wrong

  • "Can't reach the server": check the network cable or Wi-Fi. The app keeps trying and comes back by itself.
  • A window is missing: click its button on the taskbar, or Layouts → Reset to the starting layout.
  • Wrong room: only an admin can move a laptop to another room. Ask in Chat or press Help.
  • End of the day: press Sign out, or leave it; it signs out by itself at 05:00.
