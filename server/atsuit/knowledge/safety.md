# Power and safety

## Power tripped or no power
Keywords: power off, tripped, rcd, breaker, fuse, no power, socket dead, outage
1. Make sure everyone is safe first, then tell the venue's duty technician or electrician.
2. Find what tripped (RCD or breaker on the distribution board) and unplug what was added last before resetting it. If it trips again, leave it off and call the electrician.
3. Don't overload circuits: spread high loads (heaters, kettles, amps, moving lights) across circuits.
4. Only qualified people open distribution boards or connect to three-phase power.

## Cable safety
Keywords: trip hazard, cable ramp, gaffer, cable run, walkway, fire exit
1. Never run cables across fire exits or escape routes.
2. Cover cables in walkways with cable ramps or matting, or tape them flat with gaffer tape, and run them along walls where you can.
3. Uncoil cable drums fully when they carry power, or they can overheat.
4. Use only PAT-tested (or locally certified) equipment, and check plugs and cables for damage.

## Working at height and rigging
Keywords: ladder, height, rigging, truss, scaffold, tower, hanging, overhead
1. Only trained people work at height or rig, using the right equipment (podiums, towers, mobile platforms).
2. Keep the area below clear, and wear hard hats when anyone is working overhead.
3. Every item hung overhead needs a safety bond; never exceed the rated load of a truss or rigging point.

## Show stop or emergency
Keywords: emergency, evacuation, show stop, fire alarm going off, alarm, fire, evacuate, first aid, medical, incident, security
1. Know the venue's show stop procedure, who calls it, and the agreed words before doors open.
2. If the fire alarm goes, follow the venue's plan: bring up house lights, stop playback, and give the evacuation message (the venue's own, if they have one).
3. For a medical emergency, call the venue's first aiders and emergency services as the venue's procedure says; tell the stage manager so the show can stop if needed.
4. Afterwards, write it up in the show report.

## Noise levels
Keywords: hearing, loud, noise, db, decibels, spl, ear protection
1. Keep a sound level meter on the desk and know the venue's and the local noise limits.
2. Crew in loud areas should wear hearing protection.
3. Leave room for speech to be heard comfortably without being too loud for people at the front.

## Manual handling
Keywords: lifting, heavy, carry, load in, load out, flight case, back
1. Lift with your legs and keep the load close; get help for anything heavy or awkward.
2. Use trolleys and keep flight cases on their wheels.
3. Wear safety shoes during load in and load out.
