# AT-SUITE

## Captions not showing
Keywords: captions not working, no captions, subtitles, live captions, transcript, captioning
1. In the Captions panel, check your mic is picked and Send my mic is on (the button says Stop my mic).
2. Watch the level meter while someone speaks; if it doesn't move, pick the right mic or check the desk sends to the laptop.
3. Check the caption screen shows the right room.
4. In the AT-SUITE app, captions are made on the laptop; the first time, it fetches the speech model from the server, and the server listens until it has it.
5. Add names and words the speakers use to the room's vocabulary, so they come out right.

## Captions get words wrong
Keywords: wrong words, misheard, names wrong, vocabulary, accuracy, jargon
1. Add the names, brands and acronyms to the room's vocabulary in caption settings.
2. Use a clean feed from the desk (speech mics only, no music), at a good level.
3. Turn up the vocabulary boost a little if names still come out wrong.

## Timer not showing on the stage screen
Keywords: timer screen, stage timer, countdown not showing, view, speaker timer
1. Check the screen shows the room's timer view and the room is right.
2. A Blackout on the timer blanks the screens: press Blackout again to turn it off.
3. If the Clock is on, the screens show the time of day; tap the Clock again for the timer.
4. Screens update over the network; if one shows "offline", check its network.

## Sending a message to the speaker
Keywords: message, speaker message, wrap up, quick message, stage message
1. Tap a quick message under the timer to show it on the stage screens; tap it again to hide it.
2. Tick Blink to make it flash.
3. Type your own message in the message box and press Show.

## Calling for help
Keywords: help call, need help, crew, assistance, escalate, urgent
1. Use Help in the workspace, choose what's wrong, and send. The crew sees it straight away.
2. If nobody answers, it is passed on to more people automatically.
3. When it's fixed, add how it was fixed, so the AI helper can suggest it next time.

## Using the AI helper
Keywords: ai helper, chat, ask ai, assistant, helper, knowledge base, tech guide
1. Open it from Help → Ask the AI helper, or the AI helper window in the workspace. Say what's wrong in plain words.
2. Keep talking: answer its questions ("Yes", "No", or press the buttons), or tell it what you've tried.
3. Press "This fixed it" when it's fixed, so the fix is offered next time. Press "Call for help" to bring the crew.
4. Admins add what's special about the venue in Settings → AI helper → Venue notes: those come first, ahead of the built-in guide.
