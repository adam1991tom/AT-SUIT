# Staging and rigging

## Anything rigged or overhead
Keywords: rigging, rigger, rigged, hanging, flown, overhead, chain hoist, motor, points, suspended, weight limit, load, hang a light
1. Never hang, add to or move anything overhead unless you're trained and it's your job: check with the rigger or the venue's technical manager first.
2. Every point and truss has a safe working load: the rigger needs the weight of everything you add (lights, speakers, cables, screens).
3. Don't climb truss. Use the right access kit (a MEWP or a tower) with a trained operator.
4. Keep everyone out from under a load while it moves.
5. Anything that looks wrong (a twisted sling, a bent truss, a missing safety bond) is a stop: tell the rigger straight away.

### Ask job: Is this something you are trained and signed off to rig?
- Yes: Follow the rigging plan and the safe working loads, use safety bonds on everything, and have the rigger check it before anything goes up or anyone stands under it.
- No: Stop and leave it. Tell the rigger or the venue's technical manager what you need and the weight of it; they'll do it or say how.

## Truss basics
Keywords: truss, box truss, ladder truss, spigots, pins, r-clips, clamps, safety bond, ground support, totem, span
1. Truss sections join with spigots or couplers and pins: every pin in, every R-clip fitted.
2. Every fixture on truss needs its clamp done up tight and a safety bond (steel wire) around the truss.
3. Truss is rated for a load over a span: the supplier's load tables and the rigger decide what goes on it, not guesswork.
4. Ground-support towers and totems need a proper base plate, and ballast or bracing as the supplier says.

## Stage, risers and steps
Keywords: stage deck, riser, wobble, wobbly, staging, steps, stairs, handrail, stage edge, gap, trip, leg
1. A deck that rocks, a loose leg or a gap between decks: stop using that part and tell the staging crew. Don't pack it with whatever's to hand.
2. Mark the stage edge and the top and bottom of steps with white or glow tape, and light the steps.
3. Steps need a handrail; keep them clear of cables.
4. Check the stage's load rating before putting heavy kit (LED walls, sets) on it.

## Drapes and screens
Keywords: drape, curtain, backdrop, projection screen, fast fold, screen frame, flown screen, legs, masking
1. Fast-fold screens: every leg locked, and braced or weighted so they can't fall forward.
2. Keep drapes clear of hot lights and projectors, and check they're flame-retardant (there should be a certificate).
3. Flown screens and backdrops are rigging: check with the rigger before changing anything.
