# Starting the day on a tech laptop

The laptop is enrolled once by an admin. After that, each tech signs in with their own account at the start of the day.

## Signing in each morning
Open the AT-SUIT app, type your username and password, pick your room and choose **Main PC** or **Backup PC**. Chat and help calls then show your name. It resets every morning at 05:00 (venue time), or when you press **Sign out**.

## First sign-in, or a password someone else set
You have to choose a new password the first time, and whenever an admin or manager set or reset it. ChangeMe, your username and your old password aren't accepted.

## Forgot your password
Press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. An admin or manager is told and can reset it to ChangeMe; you then choose a new one at your next sign-in.

## Main PC or Backup PC
- **Main PC** is the one on the projector. Nothing ever pops up on it: no notifications of any kind.
- **Backup PC** shows your room's chat, crew-wide messages, urgent messages and help calls bottom-right, on top of everything, including full-screen slides. They never make a sound and never take the keyboard.
- Switch between the two in **This laptop**.

## The laptop says it isn't enrolled
Ask an admin. In the console, **Laptops & screens → Add laptops** shows the enrolment code; the laptop needs the server address, that code and its own name once.
