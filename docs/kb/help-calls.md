# Calling for help

## Asking for help
Use the Help menu in your workspace. The AI helper suggests a fix straight away from your venue's notes, from how past help calls were fixed and from these help articles, while the crew is on the way.

## When a fix works
Press **This fixed it** on the helper's answer. Next time anyone asks something similar, it offers that answer first.

## Nobody answered
A help call nobody has answered with **On my way** goes out again after 2 minutes, then twice more at the same gap, to every laptop and every console and workspace page on the site. A Main PC still shows nothing, and there is never any sound. It is marked **no answer** in the help lists, and backstage screens say NO ANSWER. An admin sets the minutes in Settings → General (0 turns it off).
