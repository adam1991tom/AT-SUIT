# The tech workspace

Every part of the workspace (timer, cue list, chat, captions, screens and the rest) is a window.

## Moving and docking windows
Drag a window by its title onto the guides that appear: beside another window, into its middle to add it as a tab, or along an edge of the screen. Let go anywhere else and it floats. Drag the bars between windows to resize them.

## Popping a window out
Each window can be minimised, maximised (or double-click its title), closed, or popped out (⧉) into a window of its own for a second monitor. In the AT-SUIT app a popped-out window can stay on top of everything.

## Layouts
The taskbar along the bottom has a button per window. **Layouts** saves the arrangement under a name, for example Plenary or Captions only, and puts everything back where it started. An admin can choose **Layouts → Use on every laptop** to make their arrangement every tech's starting layout.

## The top bar
The middle of the top bar shows what's live in the room: venue time, the timer, the session on now or next, how many presenters are here, connected screens, captions and the handover notes. Each one opens its window.

## Handover notes
Notes are per room and stay until someone presses Done, so the next shift sees them. Pin one to keep it at the top. Unread notes are counted on the window and in the top bar; nothing pops up.
