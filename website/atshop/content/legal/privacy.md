## Data a venue puts into AT SUITE

When a venue uses AT SUITE, the venue decides what happens to its staff accounts, crew chat, help requests, handover notes, show reports, presenter details and audit log. The venue is the controller, and we only process that data on its instructions as its processor. If you're a technician, speaker or presenter at a venue, please read that venue's privacy notice and contact the venue about your data.

Live audio is turned into captions on the technician's laptop and is never sent to us. Caption transcripts are encrypted and deleted automatically after 12 hours, or the period the venue sets. [Solicitor/adam: confirm this matches the version shipped when hosted service launches.]

## Security

Each venue's hosted server is kept separate from every other venue's. Connections are encrypted, transcripts and handover notes are encrypted at rest, passwords are stored only as hashes, and backups are encrypted. More detail is in our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).
