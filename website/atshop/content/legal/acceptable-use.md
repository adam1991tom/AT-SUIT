# Acceptable use

This page explains clause 10 of the [terms](/legal/terms).

When you use AT SUITE, including the hosted service, you must not:

- break the law, or anyone's rights, including privacy and copyright;
- record or caption people unlawfully, or without telling them where the law requires it;
- upload malware, or anything designed to harm or take over systems;
- attack, overload, probe or test the security of our systems without our written permission;
- try to reach another venue's data or server;
- remove, bypass or interfere with the licence check;
- resell or share access to AT SUITE outside your organisation unless we've agreed in writing;
- use the AI helper to make content that is illegal, hateful or harassing.

If you find a security problem, please [tell us](/contact?kind=support) privately. We'll thank you and fix it.

We may suspend a hosted venue at once if its use puts our systems or other customers at risk, and will tell you why.
