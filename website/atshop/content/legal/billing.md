# Billing, cancellation and refunds

Words in [square brackets] are still to be decided.

## Who you pay

AT SUITE is sold by our reseller, Paddle.com Market Limited, which acts as merchant of record. Paddle takes the payment, issues your invoice and handles VAT and other sales taxes. Your card statement will show Paddle. Paddle's [buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) also apply to your purchase.

## Prices and VAT

Prices on our website are in pounds sterling and exclude VAT. VAT is added at checkout where it applies. Business customers can enter a VAT number at checkout or in the billing overview.

## Renewals

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Each renewal gives you a new licence key for the next period. It appears in your account and arrives by email. With managed hosting, we install it for you.

## Changing plan

You can change plan from your account at any time:

- **A bigger plan, a switch to yearly, or adding hosting** starts straight away. You pay the difference for the rest of the current period.
- **A smaller plan** starts straight away, and the difference is credited against your next bill.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we and Paddle will email you, and Paddle will try again over the following days. Please update your card from your account. Your current licence key has 7 spare days after the paid period. After it ends, AT SUITE gives 14 days of grace before it locks, and it never locks during a show.

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time from your account (Billing → Cancel subscription). Cancelling stops the next renewal, and your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for. Nothing is deleted from your own server. For managed hosting, see the data clause in the [terms](/legal/terms).
