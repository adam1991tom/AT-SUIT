# Billing and your account

## Update your card

[Your account](/account) → **Billing** → **Update payment method**. This opens Paddle's secure page.

## Invoices

Your account → **Billing** lists every payment with a link to its PDF invoice. Invoices come from Paddle, our reseller, and include VAT where it applies. To add a VAT number or company address, use **Billing overview**.

## Change plan or add hosting

Your account → **Plan**. More laptops, a switch to yearly, or adding hosting starts at once, and you pay the difference for the rest of the period. A smaller plan is credited on your next bill. Your new key is in your account straight away.

## Cancel

Your account → **Billing** → **Cancel subscription**. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then AT SUITE gives you 14 days of grace before it locks. You can resume before the end from the same page. See [billing, cancellation and refunds](/legal/billing).

## Signing in

There's no password. Enter your email on the [sign-in page](/login) and we send you a link that works once, for 20 minutes.
