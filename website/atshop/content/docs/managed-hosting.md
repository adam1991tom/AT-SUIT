# Managed hosting

With managed hosting we run your AT SUITE server. Your techs only install the laptop app.

## Getting your server

1. Buy a plan with managed hosting from [pricing](/pricing), or add hosting to your plan under **Plan** in your account. You choose your venue's address, for example `grandhall`.
2. We create your server at `https://grandhall.<our domain>` and email you a **setup link**. It also appears in your account until setup is done. The link works once, and only it can claim your server.
3. Open the link, create your first admin account and add your rooms. Your licence key is already installed.

## What we look after

- **Updates**: we move your server to new versions outside show times. Your admins see "Updates are looked after by your AT SUITE provider".
- **Backups**: every night, encrypted, kept 7 days and 4 weeks. Caption transcripts are never backed up.
- **Renewals**: each new licence key is installed for you.
- **Monitoring**: we're told if your server stops answering.

## Your data

Your server is sealed off from every other venue's, with its own data, accounts and network. Live audio never reaches it: captions are made on the laptops. See the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).

## Moving to your own server

Download a backup from **Backups** and restore it on your own server. [Ask us](/contact?kind=support) and we'll help.
