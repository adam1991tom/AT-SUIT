What managed hosting includes

  • Your own sealed AT SUITE server. Nothing is shared with other venues: not data, not accounts, not network.
  • HTTPS everywhere, with a firewall that only accepts traffic through Cloudflare
  • Nightly encrypted backups to separate storage in the same region. We can restore on request.
  • Health monitoring, with updates done for you outside show times
  • Licence renewals installed for you
  • A Data Processing Agreement, with you as controller and us as processor (read the draft)

What never reaches our server

Live audio is turned into captions on the tech's laptop. Caption transcripts are never backed up and are deleted after 12 hours by default. Laptop screen snapshots are off by default, held only in memory while a manager looks, and never saved.

We don't promise an uptime figure yet. The target is in the draft terms, pending solicitor review.
