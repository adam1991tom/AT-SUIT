What it replaces

One subscription instead of a drawer of tools

Show timers, a crew chat app, a caption service billed by the hour, screen test tools and a spreadsheet of laptops. AT SUITE does all of it, made for show days.

⏱Show timers

A countdown on every stage screen with a cue list, quick timers, stage messages, blink and blackout, and views for stage, backstage, clock and OBS overlays.

💬Crew chat and help

Chat per room and for all crew, with files and emoji. Help calls that go to the next person if nobody answers, plus handover notes between shifts.

CCLive captions

Captions are made on the tech's laptop from its microphone, offline. Show them on audience screens or as an OBS or vMix overlay. Transcripts delete themselves after 12 hours.

🖥Screens

Pair a screen by typing its code, pick what it shows, and push test patterns (colour bars, LED tile map, sharpness) to it from the workspace.

💻Every laptop in view

See each laptop's health and what it's showing. Updates install from the server, and the venue dashboard shows which rooms are live.

✓Show-day extras

Presenter pages for slides and check-in, a pre-show health check, PDF show reports, Bitfocus Companion buttons and an AI tech guide.

Keeps working when the Wi-Fi doesn't

  • The timer and captions run on the laptop. If the network or internet drops, the show carries on, and everything catches up when it's back.

  • Licences never lock mid-show. An expired licence gets 14 days of grace, and any lock waits until no show is on.
  • Updates never happen during a show.
