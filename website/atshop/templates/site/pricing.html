Billing questions

Who takes the payment?
Our reseller Paddle.com sells AT SUITE to you. Paddle handles the card payment, invoices and VAT, so your invoice comes from Paddle. You can pay by card, and by other methods Paddle offers in your country.
What happens when I renew?
Your subscription renews by itself. A new licence key for the next period appears in your account and arrives by email. With managed hosting we install it for you. On your own server, paste it into Licence.
Can I change plan?
Yes, from your account. A bigger plan starts straight away and you pay the difference for the rest of the period. A smaller plan is credited on your next bill.
How do I cancel?
From your account, any time. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then AT SUITE gives you 14 days of grace before it locks, and it never locks during a show. Your data is kept. See billing, cancellation and refunds.
